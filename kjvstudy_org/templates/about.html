About KJV Study

A comprehensive, open source resource for studying the King James Bible

What is KJV Study?

KJV Study is a modern web application designed to help believers engage deeply with God's Word. Built on the 1769 Cambridge Edition of the King James Version, this site provides:

  • 31,102 verses from the complete KJV Bible
  • 12,330 verses with in-depth theological commentary
  • 120,858 cross-references linking related passages
  • 14,298 Strong's Concordance entries for Hebrew and Greek word studies
  • Interlinear data with original language words and definitions
  • Study guides, reading plans, biblical biographies, and thematic resources

Our goal is to make serious Bible study accessible, providing both breadth of coverage and depth of analysis while maintaining theological fidelity to historic Christian orthodoxy.

Free & Open Source

KJV Study is completely open source and will always be free.

This means:

  • Free to use - No subscriptions, no paywalls, no hidden costs
  • Transparent - All source code is publicly available
  • Community-driven - Anyone can contribute improvements
  • Portable - You can run your own copy of the entire site
  • Extensible - Build your own tools using our data and API

View the source code on GitHub:
github.com/kennethreitz/kjvstudy.org

The site is built with modern web technologies (Python, FastAPI, HTML/CSS) and all biblical data is stored in structured JSON files. This makes it easy for developers to contribute, extend, or create derivative works.

Creator

KJV Study was created by Kenneth Reitz, a software developer who wanted to create a comprehensive Bible study resource that combines:

  • Rigorous theological analysis
  • Original language scholarship
  • Historical and cultural context
  • Modern web technology
  • Open source principles

The project began as a personal study tool and has grown into a comprehensive resource for the global church. Development is ongoing, with new features and commentary added regularly.

Theological Approach

KJV Study stands within historic Christian orthodoxy, affirming the foundational doctrines of the faith:

Scripture Alone

We affirm that the sixty-six books of the Old and New Testaments are the inspired, inerrant Word of God (2 Timothy 3:16). Scripture alone is our final authority in all matters of faith and practice, wholly trustworthy and sufficient for knowing God and His will.

The Triune God

We worship one God in three persons—Father, Son, and Holy Spirit—who is sovereign over all creation, infinitely holy, just, and merciful. His purposes stand forever, and He works all things according to the counsel of His will (Ephesians 1:11).

Salvation by Grace Through Faith

We affirm that salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone (Ephesians 2:8-9). No one can earn salvation through works; it is the gift of God. Christ's substitutionary death on the cross fully satisfied divine justice, and His righteousness is imputed to believers by faith.

The Centrality of Christ

All Scripture testifies of Christ (John 5:39). From Genesis to Revelation, the Bible reveals Jesus Christ—fully God and fully man, the only mediator between God and man (1 Timothy 2:5). He is the promised Messiah, the fulfillment of God's covenant promises, and the center of redemptive history.

Our Interpretive Approach

We seek to interpret Scripture using the historic grammatical-historical method, honoring the plain meaning of the text in its original context while recognizing the Bible's divine authorship and unity. Every passage must be understood in light of its literary and historical setting, the whole counsel of God, and the redemptive arc that culminates in Christ.

We encourage every reader to test all teaching against Scripture itself, following the example of the Bereans who examined the Scriptures daily (Acts 17:11).

Privacy & Analytics

KJV Study respects your privacy:

  • No account required - Use all features without signing up
  • No tracking cookies - We don't track your browsing across sites
  • Minimal analytics - Basic usage statistics via Gauges (privacy-focused)
  • Opt-out available - Disable analytics by visiting /?kr=1
  • No data sale - We never sell or share user data

Our goal is to provide a tool for studying God's Word, not to harvest user data.
