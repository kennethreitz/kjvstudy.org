Biblical Family Tree

Major genealogies from Genesis to the Gospels

The Bible contains detailed genealogies that trace God's plan through specific family lines. These genealogies demonstrate the historical continuity of God's promises and culminate in the birth of Jesus Christ.

Genealogy of Jesus Christ

Based on Matthew 1 and Luke 3

Matthew traces Jesus's legal lineage through Joseph (his adoptive father) back to Abraham, emphasizing His right to the throne of David. Luke traces Jesus's lineage through Mary back to Adam, emphasizing His humanity and role as the second Adam.
