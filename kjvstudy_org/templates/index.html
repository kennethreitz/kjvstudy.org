The Authorized Version, commonly known as the King James Bible, stands as one of the most influential works in the English language. Commissioned by King James I of England and completed in 1611, this translation has shaped literature, law, and spiritual life for over four centuries.

This digital edition presents the 1769 Oxford Standard text with integrated commentary and cross-references, offering readers both the majesty of the original translation and modern scholarly insights.
